Cloud & DevOps Infrastructure

Can you deploy emergency hotfixes or handle rapid infrastructure migrations?

Always. We switch quickly, but animation remains work. Let us know what the deadline is as early as possible, and we will see which approach is feasible without the feathers flying off.

Can you make an animation within a small budget?

We can always look at how we can use the available budget wisely. Maybe not everything has to be completely custom. Perhaps the story can be made shorter, simpler or modular. We say honestly what is and is not feasible.

How are cloud architecture and software projects priced?

That depends on the length, style, technique, amount of illustration work, voice-over, music and the number of versions. A short motion graphic is different from a complete 3D product film. That is why we prefer to provide a clear budget based on your question rather than a meaningless starting amount.

How long does it take to create an animation?

That depends on length, technique, style and complexity. A short social animation can be created relatively quickly. An extensive 3D animation or explainer video with many scenes requires more preparation and production time. After an initial conversation, we make a feasible plan together.

How many feedback rounds are included?

We will clearly agree on this in advance. We usually plan one or more feedback moments per phase. This way the process remains clear and we prevent the color of a sock in scene two from only becoming a topic of discussion during the final editing.

What does your software development sprint cycle look like?

We usually start with the goal, the target group and the message. This is followed by script, style development, storyboard, animation, sound design and delivery. You watch at logical moments. No weeks of radio silence and then suddenly a complete animation on the table, because you are really part of the process.

Can you adapt an existing animation?

Often yes. But that does depend on the program used. Think of new texts, different formats, extra scenes, a new voice-over or a translation. Whether it can be done easily depends on the available source files and the way in which the original production is structured.

Can you animate existing illustrations or designs?

Yep! We can work with existing illustrations, corporate identity elements, infographics, presentations, designs or previously created content. We first check whether the material is suitable for movement. Then we set it up. ### ###

Can you divide a long animation into short videos?

Sure. We can build the content modularly from the start. In addition to the main video, this creates a series of short videos for social media, presentations, websites or campaigns. Or to show to your mother-in-law.

Can you deliver one animation in multiple formats?

Yes. We can translate a main video into vertical, square and horizontal versions. We can also create short snippets, teasers, pre-rolls and advertisements.

Can you add subtitles?

We deliver videos with embedded subtitles and with a separate subtitle file. Depending on the desired platform.

Can you make an animation without sound?

But of course. For social media, a lot of content is viewed without sound. That is why we can create animations that remain clear even without voice-over or music, for example with text, subtitles and visual explanations.

Can you add music and sound design?

That too! Music sets the atmosphere. Sound design makes movement believable and gives an animation extra rhythm. We look for suitable sound and arrange the correct licenses where necessary.

Can you arrange a voice-over?

Yes. We can help with the voice-over text, find a suitable voice and supervise the recording. From warm and human to businesslike, dry or outspoken. ABN or Brabant.

Do you also make a storyboard?

Always! In a storyboard we record what happens in each scene, what you see and what you hear. This way everyone knows where the animation is going before we actually set everything in motion.

Do you also write the script for an animation?

Yep. You don't have to come in with a finished script. A conversation, rough text, presentation or head full of loose thoughts is often enough for us. We find out what really needs to be said and write a script that sounds clear and flows smoothly.

How long should an animated video be?

As short as possible and as long as necessary. A social animation can make its point within six to fifteen seconds. An explanation video often lasts one to three minutes. The right length depends on your story, target group and channel.

Do you make animations for social media?

We create short animations for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube and online advertisements. Vertical, square, horizontal and everything in between.

Do you make animations for websites?

Naturally. We create animated headers, short loops, icons, product explanations, interface animations and other moving parts for websites and apps.

Do you make logo animations?

Definitely! From a subtle logo reveal to a distinctly animated brand identity. Useful for video intros, social content, presentations, events and websites.

Can you also develop a new animation style?

Yes. Even if there is no fixed visual style yet, we develop a direction that suits the brand, the target group and the message. Think of style elements, illustrations, typography, use of color and the way everything moves.

Can you make an animation in our corporate identity?

Gladly even! We use existing colors, font and other brand elements and develop our own visual animation style. We do it all ourselves. This way the animation feels like part of your brand and not like an alien bird.

What is the difference between 2D and 3D animation?

In 2D animation we work with flat illustrations, shapes, typography and graphic elements. In 3D animation we build objects and environments with depth. 3D is not necessarily better. Well different.

Do you make 2D and 3D animations?

Yes, both. 2D often works well for explanations, illustrations, characters and graphic storytelling. 3D is strong for products, spaces, technical processes and subjects that you want to show from every angle.

Do you make explainer videos?

Yes. We make animated videos that explain step by step how a product, service, organization or process works. Clear enough to understand. Strong enough not to be forgotten immediately.

Can you explain a complicated story with animation?

Gladly even! Telling a difficult story easily is exactly what warms us up. We translate complex material into clear copy, strong illustrations and animation that people understand and remember.

When is animation a good choice?

Animation works well when a story is difficult to film, difficult to explain or not yet visible. Think of a technical process, an abstract service, a new product, a sensitive message or a plan about which there are now seventeen PowerPoints.

What technology stacks and cloud providers does elansinfotech support?

We make 2D animations, 3D animations, motion graphics, explainer videos, product animations, character animations, logo animations and short animations for social media. In other words: everything that needs to move, explain or stick.

A compact production can be achieved within a few days. A larger film with casting, multiple shooting days, animation or many versions requires a little more time. We make a plan in advance that is correct.

What does an end-to-end full-stack development project look like?

We start with the goal, the target group and the message. This is followed by concept, script or shooting plan, production preparation, recording, editing and delivery.

How long should a corporate film be?

That depends on where the film ends up and what it has to say. An online brand film can do its work within one minute. A substantive case film or explanation video may sometimes take longer. In any case, we won't keep it any longer because there are a lot of nice shots.

Do you deliver videos in different formats?

That too. We supply horizontal, vertical and square versions for websites, presentations, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube and online advertisements.

Do you make videos in multiple languages?

Can you combine photography with video?

Yes. It is often smart to take photography with you during a shooting day. This way you get more content from the same location, planning and people.

How big is your film crew?

As big as necessary. A compact crew is often enough for a small interview. We scale up with the right makers and specialists for a commercial, campaign film or production at multiple locations.

Can you arrange voiceovers for video?

Can you arrange actors, models or presenters?

Yes. If production requests it, we can help with casting, briefing and guidance.

Do you help people who don't like being in front of the camera?

Absolutely. Most people don't relax and talk to a lens every day. We take our time, give clear instructions and ensure that the conversation remains natural.

Can you film interviews?

Yes. We help with preparation, formulate questions and provide a setting in which people can talk normally. During editing we remove repetition and detours, without anyone suddenly sounding as if they were raised by a communications manual.

Can you combine video and animation?

Gladly even! Animation can enhance a film with graphic elements, occasional text, data visualizations, explanations, product details or complete animated scenes.

Can you edit existing video material?

Do you make videos for social media?

Yes. Social video requires pace, a strong 'hook' as an opening and a clear reason not to scroll straight through. We take this into account from the concept onwards.

Do you make corporate films?

Yes. For us, a corporate film is not a tour of all departments. We look for the story that makes an organization interesting, credible or distinctive. And we do that in our own way.

We make brand films, corporate films, commercials, product videos, employer branding videos, campaign videos, interviews, testimonials and social videos.

Copyright and usage rights

What about copyright on AI-generated images?

The legislation and conditions of use surrounding AI continue to evolve. That is why we examine for each project which tool, source files, licensing conditions and risks suit the desired use. ## ##

Do you use AI in productions?

Sometimes we use AI as a tool for research, concept development, previsualization, image editing or other parts of the process.

Who is responsible for substantive claims?

The client remains responsible for the accuracy of legal, medical, technical and commercial claims. We help to formulate the content clearly and comprehensibly, but we are not automatically the content specialist or ultimately legally responsible.

Who is responsible for supplied images?

The client is responsible for the permission and rights of supplied logos, photos, videos, music, texts and other files. If in doubt, we are happy to take a look before something is used in production. This prevents duplication of work.

Can you sign a confidentiality agreement?

Yep. We can sign an NDA for confidential information, products, campaigns and internal plans.

Can you show our project in your portfolio?

We like to show completed work on our website, social channels and in presentations. We make separate agreements for confidential productions, embargoes or white-label collaborations.

How are the rights of a voice-over arranged?

The rights to a voice-over also depend on the use. An internal video has different conditions than a commercial that is used as an advertisement for a long time. We will agree on this in advance.

How are the rights of actors and models regulated?

We make agreements in advance about channels, duration, region and possible exclusivity. This is usually done via a quitclaim or separate agreement.

Do you use stock images?

Sometimes. If stock material adds something to the concept or makes production feasible, we can use it. We pay attention to style, quality and licensing conditions.

Can we supply music ourselves?

Yes, as long as you demonstrably have the correct usage rights.

Can you arrange music licensing?

Yes. We look for suitable music and arrange the license for the agreed use. We make the costs and conditions transparent in advance.

Can we use individual images from a video?

Some items may only be licensed as part of the complete video. Let us know in advance which additional applications are desired, such as photography, stills or individual clips. We are happy to think along!

Can we make adjustments to the video or animation ourselves?

Only when this has been agreed and you have the correct files, programs and rights.

What are source files?

These are the work files with which the production is built up. Think of editing projects, animation files, individual illustrations, 3D models, audio layers and linked files.

Do we get the source files?

Source files are not a standard part of the delivery. In some cases they can be transferred additionally for a fee. This depends on the production, software used, licenses and agreements.

Can usage rights be extended later?

Often yes. When a campaign runs longer, goes to other countries or is distributed through additional channels, additional rights and licenses can be arranged.

Can we use a video worldwide?

Only when worldwide use has been agreed and all parts used have been properly licensed.

Can we use the content for paid advertising?

When paid media is part of the agreements: yes. When a video was originally created for organic use only, paid advertising may require an additional license.

Can we use the video online indefinitely?

This depends on the agreed usage rights and any licenses for music, actors, voice-overs, stock images and other components. We determine in advance what does and does not fall under production.

Why are usage rights agreed in advance?

Because use affects the necessary rights and licenses. An internal presentation is different from a national television campaign, a paid social advertisement or worldwide use for an indefinite period.

Do we automatically receive all copyrights?

No, not automatically. A full transfer of copyright must be explicitly agreed. In many cases, a broad user license is more practical and sufficient.

What is the difference between copyright and right of use?

In principle, copyright belongs to the creator of the work. Usage rights give a client permission to use the work in agreed ways.

Who owns a video or animation after delivery?

After full payment, the client receives the rights to use the video or animation. This does not automatically mean that all copyrights are fully transferred. Use and ownership are not exactly the same from a legal perspective.
